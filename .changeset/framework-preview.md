The `preview` command now runs `@redocly/framework` and accepts the `pro`, `scale` and `enterprise` plans with the `--plan` option. The `--product` option is deprecated and ignored: the preview no longer launches `@redocly/realm`, `@redocly/redoc`, `@redocly/revel` or `@redocly/reef`.
